Your pulse account

Derived in-tab from a device seed (WebCrypto CSPRNG, stretched with pbkdf2). This is your in-browser wallet — it never leaves this origin, and every term, fact and verdict you weave is signed with it (secp256k1 ECDSA). Clear site data and it is gone.

Vote — facts & terms to verify

Cast a signed verdict on any woven CID. Mode: signed-verdict — knitweb-vank personhood gating is not available in-browser, so this is pubkey-weighted: one vote per distinct signer (your account is the signer here), latest verdict per author counting. Personhood-gated one-person-one-vote is the documented upgrade.
